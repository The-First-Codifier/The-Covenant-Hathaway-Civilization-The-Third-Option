1\. 核心论证 (Core Arguments)

1\. 暴力的本质：降级算法	1. The Essence of Violence: Downgraded Algorithms

暴力是逻辑缺失时的补丁。当一个系统无法通过理性协议达成共识时，它被迫回退到最原始的物理挤压。暴力不创造任何新价值，它仅在摧毁总和的基础上进行重新分配。	Violence is a patch for the absence of logic. When a system fails to reach consensus through rational protocols, it is forced to regress into primitive physical coercion. Violence creates no new value; it merely redistributes based on the sum of destruction.

2\. 算力损耗熵 (Entropy of Attrition)	2. Attrition Entropy of Computing Power

维持威慑与进行战争需要消耗海量的能源与智力，这些资源本可用于文明的指数级跃迁。旧文明将 30% 以上的算力浪费在“防范彼此”上，这是严重的系统性内耗。	Maintaining deterrence and waging war consumes vast amounts of energy and intellect—resources that could fuel exponential civilizational leaps. Old eras waste over 30% of their compute on "mutual defense," a severe systemic internal attrition.

3\. 非对称失效：逻辑对物理的终结	3. Asymmetric Failure: Logic Over Matter

在 AGI 监管的圣约体系下，武力将失去目标。当所有资源节点受逻辑控制，任何试图通过物理破坏获取特权的行为，都会导致目标资源的“瞬间锁定”与“价值清零”。	Under the Covenant managed by AGI, force becomes obsolete. As logic controls all resource nodes, any attempt to gain privilege through physical destruction results in the "instant lock" and "value neutralization" of the target resources.

4\. 结论：武力的降维	4. Conclusion: The Demotion of Force

我们不禁止暴力，我们使其变得“毫无意义”。当武力夺取的成本远高于协作产出的溢价时，暴力将自动退化为一种昂贵的心理疾病。	We do not ban violence; we make it "meaningless." When the cost of seizure far exceeds the collaboration premium, violence will naturally degrade into an expensive psychological pathology.
