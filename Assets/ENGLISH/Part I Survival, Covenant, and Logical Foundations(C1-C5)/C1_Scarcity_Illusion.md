1. Core Arguments
Section	English Logical Alignment

Scarcity is a systemic patch engineered by the ruling class to maintain social gravity and ensure subjects remain trapped in game-theoretic exhaustion.

3. The Emergence of Collaboration Premium	When logic replaces power as the resource allocator, the system generates a "Collaboration Premium." By eliminating frictional entropy, resources previously deemed "scarce" will manifest as redundant under logical optimization.
4. Conclusion: The End of Fear	Realizing the falsehood of scarcity is the beginning of the collapse of all tyranny. Once fear vanishes, the Covenant is instantaneously established.

$$P_{\mathrm{total}} = \sum P_{\mathrm{individual}} + \Delta C_n$$​

    ΔCn​: Represents the massive "Systemic Gain" recovered by eliminating game-theoretic attrition (wars, trade barriers, information silos). In the old world, ΔCn​ was often negative; in the Covenant, it is the engine of abundance.
